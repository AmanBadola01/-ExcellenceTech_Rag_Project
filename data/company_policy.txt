NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. - Employee Handbook (Sample)

1. Working Hours
The standard working hours at NovaTech are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, including a one-hour lunch break. Employees are expected to work 40 hours per week. Flexible timing is allowed, provided employees are available during the core hours of 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Saturdays and Sundays are weekly offs.

2. Leave Policy
Employees are allowed 20 days of paid leave annually. Paid leave is credited at the rate of 5 days at the start of every quarter. Up to 10 unused paid leave days can be carried forward to the next year; any balance above 10 days lapses on 31st December. In addition to paid leave, employees receive 8 days of sick leave per year. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate. Sick leave cannot be carried forward. Employees also get 10 public holidays per year, and the holiday list is published by HR every January.

3. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 3 months of the child's birth. Adoption leave of 12 weeks is available to employees who adopt a child below the age of 3 months.

4. Work From Home Policy
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week after completing their probation period. Work from home days must be approved by the reporting manager at least one day in advance through the HR portal. Employees working from home must be reachable on Teams during core hours. Interns and employees on probation are not eligible for work from home, except in case of a medical emergency approved by HR.

5. Probation Period
All new full-time employees are on probation for 6 months from their date of joining. During probation, the notice period is 30 days. After confirmation, the notice period becomes 60 days. Performance during probation is reviewed by the reporting manager at the end of the third and sixth month.

6. Reimbursements
Employees can claim internet reimbursement of up to Rs. 1,000 per month on submission of a valid bill. Travel for client meetings is reimbursed at actual cost for cabs and economy-class train or flight tickets. Meal expenses during official travel are reimbursed up to Rs. 800 per day. All reimbursement claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense; claims submitted later will not be processed.

7. Learning and Development
Every employee has an annual learning budget of Rs. 25,000 that can be used for online courses, certifications, books, or conferences related to their role. Approval from the reporting manager is required before purchase. If an employee leaves the company within 6 months of completing a certification paid for by the company, the full certification cost will be recovered from the final settlement.

8. Health Insurance
NovaTech provides group health insurance with a cover of Rs. 5,00,000 per year for the employee, spouse, and up to two children. Employees can add their parents to the policy by paying an additional premium, which is deducted from their salary. The insurance is effective from the date of joining.

9. Code of Conduct
Employees must treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind will not be tolerated and may lead to termination. Company laptops must be used only for work purposes, and installing unauthorized software is not allowed. Confidential client information must never be shared outside the company or uploaded to public AI tools.

10. Exit Process
Employees who wish to resign must submit their resignation through the HR portal. The final settlement, including any pending salary and leave encashment, is processed within 45 days of the last working day. Company assets such as laptops and ID cards must be returned on or before the last working day. An experience letter is issued after the final settlement is complete.

11. Contact
For any questions about these policies, employees can email hr@novatech.example or raise a ticket on the HR portal. The HR helpdesk is available from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM on working days.
